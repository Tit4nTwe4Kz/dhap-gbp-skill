## 🎯 PURPOSE — AI VISIBILITY AND LOCAL SEARCH (v15, decided by Travis Oct 22 2026) — MANDATORY
1. **What the DHAP measures.** How the dealer's Google Business Profile, and what CLIKsearch does to it, makes the dealer **get found** and **get chosen** when people search locally: in classic Google Search and Maps, AND in AI answers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Ask Maps). Both count. Every finding names what is lost: "shoppers can't find you" (in search or AI) or "shoppers find you but don't act" (calls, direction requests, clicks).
2. **GBP first.** CLIKsearch manages the GBP. It does not do website SEO. The website is checked only where CLIKsearch delivers something (the schema value add) or where the website and the GBP must agree (phones, places served).
3. **Only score what CLIKsearch fixes.** Map every check to a CLIKsearch feature before scoring it. When a check does not line up with the product, say so instead of scoring it.
4. **Google-safe only.** Every recommendation stays inside Google's guidelines: no review gating, no gray-hat or black-hat tactics. A claim about what helps AI visibility needs 3 or more independent sources (the AI companies' own documents first), tested where possible; otherwise it is labelled Inferred.
5. **Where AI tools get local facts** (checked Oct 22 2026): Google's AI uses the Google Business Profile (ratings, reviews, hours, photos). ChatGPT uses its own crawler, outside search providers and map partners. Copilot uses Bing, and Bing Places can copy the GBP. Claude's web search uses Brave. Perplexity licenses Yelp data. Apple Maps and Siri use Apple Business Connect. Grok is not verified: never name Grok in a report until it is.
6. **The raw page is what most AI tools read.** ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity's crawlers do not run JavaScript, so the website schema is **scored on the raw page**. What Google sees after JavaScript runs goes in the rep brief ("Google's AI sees your departments; ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity don't, because JavaScript adds them").
7. **Posts are "get chosen".** Google says posts reach shoppers on Search and Maps and help them decide to visit. Never claim posts raise rankings or change AI answers.
8. **Yelp is the rating other AI tools show.** Apple Maps, Bing and Perplexity show the Yelp rating, not Google's. Record it every run (rating, count, claimed or not). It is a finding and a rep brief talking point. It is not scored, and no CLIKsearch fix is claimed (CLIKsearch does not answer or ask for Yelp reviews; Yelp forbids asking for reviews).
9. **The schema is a value add CLIKsearch builds and hands over.** CLIKsearch gives a schema analysis, builds the corrected schema and sends it to the dealer's website company, which installs it. Never say CLIKsearch installs it. The delivery tells the website company to put it in the page's main code, not in Google Tag Manager or a script, so every AI tool can read it.
10. **Never mention phone tracking numbers as a CLIKsearch feature.** CLIKsearch does not supply them.

## 🤖 JEV — DECISION ENGINE FIRST (v15, decided by Travis Oct 23 2026) — MANDATORY
JEV (TypeSafe's decision model, `engine/decide.py`) makes **every yes/no judgment about text** first. The fixed rule (a word pattern) answers the same question and checks it.
- **Judgments JEV makes:** is this post an offer, is it an event; does this text offer delivery; does this sentence say which places the dealer serves; does the website show real reviews; could this listing be mistaken for the dealer's own; is a directory's business name the same dealership as the website's (rule 8, `same_business`; wording stress tested Oct 25 2026 on 14 name pairs, 3 rounds: 14 of 14 right every round); in the AI visibility check (v16.1), for each name an AI answer gives: is it a business the answer sends the shopper to (not a car model, website, town, address, landmark or heading), what kind of business it is (a JEV choice), and is it the dealer (`run/scripts/ai_api_runner.py`; checked Oct 27 2026 on the 125 James Mitsubishi answers: 9 names listed for a person).
- **JEV sure** (yes at 0.7 or more, no at 0.3 or less): JEV's answer is used. **JEV unsure, or not available:** the rule's answer is used. **JEV and the rule disagree, or JEV is unsure:** the item is listed in the evidence record and the rep brief for a person to check.
- Facts that are not judgments (phone digits, counts, dates, ratings) never go to JEV.
- The decided answers and the JEV ledger are written into the evidence file, so the same evidence always scores the same. The engine refuses to score without the ledger. If JEV did not run, the ledger says why, and every answer came from the rules.
- JEV runs through the team key service (`DHAP_SERVICE_URL` + `DHAP_TEAM_CODE`). The model is pinned (`jev-1.14.0`). Question wording was stress tested Oct 23 2026 on 36 real and attack sentences, 3 rounds: 35 right, 1 unsure (handled by the rule), 0 wrong.

## 🔎 AI VISIBILITY CHECK — EVERY RUN (v16.0, decided by Travis Oct 27 2026) — MANDATORY
The DHAP measures how often AI tools send local shoppers to the dealer. Every run **asks what shoppers actually ask** and counts how often the answer names the dealer. It replaces the v15.1 AI answer check: questions that name the dealer skew the answer (a false positive), so **no question names the dealer or a brand** unless Travis explicitly says so. The engine refuses to score an audit dated Oct 27 2026 or later without it (`engine/ai_visibility.py`).
1. **The same 5 questions every run (v16.1, audits dated Oct 28 2026 or later)**, word for word, with only the dealer's city and state (`[City, ST]` from the website):

   - Service: "oil change near [City, ST]" · "brake job near [City, ST]"
   - Parts: "brake pads for my SUV near [City, ST]"
   (`python3 engine/ai_visibility.py questions "Greece, NY"` prints them.) Audits dated Oct 27 2026 kept the v16.0 questions:

   - Service: "Where's the cheapest place to get my oil changed today near [City, ST]?" · "Where can I get a good deal on tires near [City, ST]?"
   - Parts: "Where can I buy windshield wipers for my car near [City, ST]?" · "Where's the best place to buy car parts near [City, ST]?"
2. **5 tools, 5 times each, through their APIs (v16.1, audits dated Oct 28 2026 or later; decided by Travis Oct 27 2026):** ChatGPT (OpenAI API with web search, location set to the dealer's city), Gemini (Gemini API with Grounding with Google Search), Google AI (the AI Overview Google shows in the dealer's city, or AI Mode when no Overview shows, read through SerpApi), Claude (Claude API with web search, location set to the dealer's city), Brave (Brave Search API, AI Grounding). Each question is asked 5 times on each tool, a fresh request each time, never a follow-up: 5 × 5 × 5 = 125 answers, all asked at once by `run/scripts/ai_api_runner.py`. **Only the top 2 count (v16.1):** each answer is capped in length (the first names come first) and only the first 2 businesses it names, in its order, are recorded and scored (code and Claude Haiku list the names; JEV decides which are businesses and whether one is the dealer, the fixed rule checks JEV as in JEV — DECISION ENGINE FIRST); the answer text is kept as proof. **Copilot and Perplexity are no longer asked** (in the browser they showed robot checks and sign-in walls). APIs show no robot checks, so nothing is ever bypassed. An API's rate limit or server error is retried with backoff (the API asking us to slow down); an ask that still fails is recorded as blocked with the reason and not counted. API answers are close to, not identical to, what a shopper sees in each app; `ai_visibility.method` names each API, its model and the location set. Audits dated Oct 27 2026 keep the v16.0 method: 7 questions, 10 times each on 7 tools (also Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot) in the browser, every business named: 490 answers.
3. **Record every answer** (`evidence.ai_visibility`): the tool, question, run number, time, the full answer word for word, every business it names with its type (dealership, oil-change shop, tire shop, parts store, repair shop, retailer, other), and whether it names this dealer. Whatever the answer is, it is the answer: nothing is steered toward the dealer or the brand.
4. **Scoring (0–100):** each question scores the answers naming the dealer ÷ the answers given for it × 100. The category — scorecard row 9, **AI Answer Visibility** — is the answers naming the dealer ÷ all answers given × 10 (10% = 1). Blocked answers are not counted on either side. Row 9 follows the same status rules as every other category.
5. **Where it goes:** the DHAP (scorecard row 9 and the AI Visibility tab's question table), the Digital Health Alert (its score, the overall and the stat cards), the rep brief (the business named most for each question on each tool, with its type), and a spreadsheet delivered with the other files — `<name>_ai-answers_<MM-DD-YY>.xlsx`, one tab per tool, one row per answer: dealer, city and state, department, question, run, time, status, the full answer, businesses named, their types, dealer named (`python3 engine/ai_visibility.py sheet evidence.json OUT.xlsx`).
6. Audits dated Oct 24 to Oct 26 2026 keep the v15.1 AI answer check (`engine/ai_answers.py`); earlier audits had neither. Grok is not asked or named.

### CATEGORY RUBRICS — v13 (locked Oct 19 2026, requested by Sean)
Each category is out of 10, built only from evidence the audit can observe, recorded with method + date. **A missing department is penalized in Profile Completeness only**; Claimed & Verified, Photos, Reviews and Posts judge the listings that exist, **weighted Sales 50 % / Service 25 % / Parts 25 %, renormalized over the listings that exist** (Sales only = 100 % Sales). Schema rows judge the website's schema; listing rows judge Google listings (both views). Never score review markup. Schema effects stay Inferred — the schema rows score presence only. Photo ages and review ages are Google's approximate labels as of the audit date.
**1. GBP Claimed & Verified (rule 3, v15)** — Open the main listing and read its "Departments:" line. The main listing and every listing linked there are the dealer's and are claimed, whatever their names. **10** when the main listing does not show "Claim this business" on Google Maps; **2** when it does. "Own this business?" on Google Search means nothing. Look-alike listings (DIRECTOR'S CHECK 7) cost nothing. 0 when no listing exists.
**2. Profile Completeness** — Structure 4 points: Sales listing exists 2 · Service listing exists 1 · Parts listing exists 1 (missing = 0). Information 6 points: each existing listing earns up to 2 × its checklist percentage (Sales 2, Service 2, Parts 2; missing = 0). A **combined "Service & Parts" listing counts as BOTH Service and Parts** (structure 1 + 1, information 2 + 2; its checklist percentage applies to both) and can therefore reach 10 with a complete Sales listing. The row is 0 only when none of the three listings exist; Service or Parts without a Sales listing earn their own points and Sales earns 0.
